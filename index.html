Our priority is you

Why Affordable Housing Makes Sense

Lower cost

Housing built to fit a tight budget. There is no security deposit, and the home is furnished, so you have no furniture to buy.

Easier to get in

Many rentals turn people away over credit checks, deposits, and monthly income requirements. We don’t run credit, and we don’t ask for a deposit.

A better quality of life

Lower housing costs free up money for healthcare, education, and food, and give you steadier ground to build on.

What we do

Housing solutions for displaced individuals

Finding affordable housing can feel impossible when the cost of living keeps climbing. We understand the pressure of not having a place to sleep at night, and we built Villa St. Rose to answer it.

Every home is newly renovated and kept clean, with a private fenced backyard and shared spaces where residents connect and support one another.
